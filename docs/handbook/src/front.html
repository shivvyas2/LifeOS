Almanac

Engineering Handbook

Shiv Vyas · Revision E

Almanac is a personal iOS app that gathers every signal from the devices you already wear and carry (Apple Watch, iPhone, Whoop, Fitbit, a Renpho scale, your bank, your calendar) into one local store, scores your life across nine sectors, and puts a coach called LIFO on top of all of it. This handbook is the single reference for how it is built, why it is built that way, what keeps it fast and cheap, and what it does when things go wrong. It is written to be studied: every chapter ends with a reading order through the code and questions to check yourself against.
